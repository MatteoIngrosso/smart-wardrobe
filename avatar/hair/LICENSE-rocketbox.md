# Hair textures and shapes: Microsoft Rocketbox

The files in this folder (`<style>.json`, `<style>.webp`) are made by `tools/build-hair.py` from the male heads of
Microsoft Rocketbox (https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox): the hair painted on each head's scalp
(`<id>_head_color.tga`) and its hair cards (`<id>_opacity_color.tga`), reshaped for our head and stored as
brightness + coverage. Source heads: buzz Male_Adult_12, short 20, fade 08, sidepart 05, crop 07, slick 09,
curly 06, wavy 01, long 02. Their licence:
